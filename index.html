About the project

About AKLATAYA

A student-designed digital library prototype that brings resource discovery, metadata, research support, and cultural heritage into one green-and-white interface.

Our mission

To make learning resources and cultural information easier to discover, understand, and access through an organized and user-friendly digital library experience.

Our vision

A learning community where information is easier to find, knowledge is responsibly shared, and digital heritage is respected and preserved.

Intended users

Students, teachers, librarians, researchers, and lifelong learners looking for educational materials and cultural information.

Repository type

A centralized digital library discovery portal prototype. A production version could connect to a repository platform such as Omeka S or DSpace, with storage and administrative controls.

Technology Features

How the proposed digital library could operate.

Interface

HTML for structure, CSS for visual design, and JavaScript for navigation, filtering, search, and interactive controls.

Metadata

Dublin Core-inspired fields: title, creator, subject, description, publisher, date, format, language, identifier, and rights.

File formats

PDF, EPUB, HTML, TXT, JPG, PNG, TIFF, MP3, WAV, MP4, and other supported preservation or access formats.

Implementation note: This standalone HTML prototype does not provide server-side storage, user accounts, database management, or file uploads. Those features require a repository backend and authorized content.

Legal Rights & Copyright

Access information responsibly and respect intellectual property.

Copyright principles

  • Respect ownership: Materials may belong to authors, publishers, photographers, institutions, or other rights holders.
  • Check licenses: Confirm whether an item is public domain, openly licensed, restricted, or available only under specific terms.
  • Attribute sources: Cite the creator and source when required. Attribution does not by itself grant permission to reuse copyrighted material.
  • Use authorized copies: Do not upload, redistribute, or modify protected files without permission or another valid legal basis.
  • Protect privacy: Review privacy, consent, and ethical considerations before making personal or sensitive archival materials accessible.
  • Verify rights metadata: A real repository should document rights statements and access restrictions for each item.

This prototype links to external institutions and does not claim ownership of their content. Users should verify each item's rights and access conditions at the source.
